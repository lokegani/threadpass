Palette check

Runs the computable colour checks against the chart colours actually defined in assets/css/styles.css — it reads the live CSS variables rather than a copy of the hex values, so it cannot drift out of date when you change them.

What this does not check. Two of the six rules are structural, not measurable from colour alone: that categorical hues are assigned in a fixed order and never cycled, and that the values come from a documented palette. Those stay your responsibility.

This tools folder is a development aid. It is safe to delete before deploying.
